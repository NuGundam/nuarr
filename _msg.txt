How files flow: top-down two-lane chart, page loads in milliseconds

- pipeline.graph(): picture 1 is top-down. Lane A "Imported or upgraded"
  (an upgrade is a new arr file id, so a new file) rides the fresh band for
  24 h; lane B "Already in the library" (six standing checks, a rule change,
  the remedy verbs) re-enters behind it. Both meet at Ready to plan, then
  the four facts, plan, gate, encode/passthrough, commit, Subtitle OCR /
  Subtitle maker / Subtitle fixes, Watchable. Fresh-band counts added.
- pipeline._slow(): the historical counts (1.5-2.5 s together) memoised
  5 min; the live graph builds in ~0.2 s.
- route(): lights the new node ids (fresh, elig, f_*, submake, subs, watch).
- /api/pipeline/route serves one file's route alone (tour + trace).
- the page shell is drawn once; the 15 s poll rewrites count texts in
  place, so typing in the trace box is never lost and the animations never
  restart. pipeSvg draws dir:'down' graphs with pill labels and side
  detours for jumps over a row.
- the order chain names the Subtitle maker.
